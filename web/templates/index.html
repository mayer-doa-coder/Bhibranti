How to read this

Why they disagree so much. These models count words and compare text. They cannot look up a fact. On hard questions they score near 0.50, which is a coin toss, so a split vote is the normal result.

Why row 1 is not always right. The list is ordered by past performance, not by confidence today. The best model here still gets about 4 hard questions in 10 wrong.

What would fix it. A model that can line the answer up against the passage and check the parts that matter — that is the next step of the project.
